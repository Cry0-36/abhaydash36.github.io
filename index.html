P-01 / Final-year project

EV battery pack thermal management

JUN – OCT 2026 · TEAM OF 3 · FUSION 360 · COMSOL · NEWARE BTS · EXCEL
  • Peak cell temperature cut by about 50% at 2C (73.5 to 37.1 °C) and 37% at 1.5C (62.7 to 39.5 °C) with an OM37 PCM jacket against a bare cell, tested on a Neware cycler.
  • Cell-to-cell spread reduced from about 11 K to under 2 K. PCM melting point verified at about 43 °C by logging a 5 g sample over 34 min.
  • Designed a two-piece detachable CPCM jacket (3 mm PCM layer), and built a transient 3D electrochemical-thermal COMSOL model plus an Excel heat-generation tool to screen designs.
